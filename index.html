Recording best practices

Lighting & contrast

Put your light source in front of you, not behind. Backlit silhouettes lose landmark tracking fast.

Camera angle

A clean 90° side profile is what lets the engine measure depth, lockout, and body line correctly.

Full body in frame

Head to toes visible through the entire range of motion — partial framing is the top cause of rejected clips.
